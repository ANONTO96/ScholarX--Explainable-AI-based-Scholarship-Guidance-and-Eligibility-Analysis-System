Announcement: Chinese Government Scholarship (CGS) / CSC Type A
2025-12-19 00:43

The Government of the People’s Republic of China is pleased to announce that Bangladeshi students are invited to apply for the Chinese Government Scholarship (CGS) – Type A, administered by the China Scholarship Council (CSC).

This prestigious fully funded scholarship is open to outstanding Bangladeshi students wishing to pursue the following programs in Chinese universities:

  • Bachelor’s Degree
  • Master’s Degree
  • Doctoral (PhD) Degree
  • One-Year Chinese Language Program

Scholarship Coverage

The Chinese Government Scholarship (CGS) provides comprehensive financial support, including:

  • Full tuition fees
  • On-campus accommodation
  • Comprehensive medical insurance
  • Monthly living allowance (approximately CNY 2,500–3,500, depending on program level)
  • International round-trip airfare

Application Procedure

All applicants must complete their application online through the official China Scholarship Council website:www.campuschina.org

Applicants are advised to carefully follow the instructions and submit all required documents within the stipulated time.

Application Deadline: January 10, 2026

Further Information

For official guidance, clarification, or inquiries regarding the application process, applicants are encouraged to contact the Embassy of the People’s Republic of China in Bangladesh.

The Chinese Government Scholarship offers an excellent opportunity for Bangladeshi students to pursue higher education in China and to strengthen people-to-people exchange and educational cooperation between China and Bangladesh.
